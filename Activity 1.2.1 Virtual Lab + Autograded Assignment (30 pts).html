Part A: Inside a sarcomere

A sarcomere is the contracting unit of a muscle. Thin filaments (actin) slide past thick filaments (myosin). Change the conditions and watch what happens.

Sarcomere length: 2.20 µm Shortened: 0%

Try this: (1) Turn calcium on and watch. (2) Turn calcium off. (3) Turn calcium on, wait until it shortens, then drag ATP to 0%, then turn calcium off.

Part B: Biceps and triceps

Muscles only pull, and they work in pairs. Set how hard each muscle contracts and see how the forearm moves. The origin stays put; the insertion moves.
